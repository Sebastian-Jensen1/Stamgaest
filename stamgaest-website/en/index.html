Stamgæst writes the replies to your Google reviews. You read them and press send.

Guests notice whether a place replies. But after a long shift, it's rarely what you feel like doing. Stamgæst fetches your reviews, drafts a reply in your tone, and only posts it once you've approved it.

How it works

  1. Connect your café

    Sign in with the Google account that manages your café's Business Profile. You can revoke access at any time.

  2. Read the drafts

    New reviews show up with a draft reply, written in the tone you've chosen. Edit the text if you like.

  3. Approve and send

    The reply is posted to Google when you press send. Nothing is posted without you.

See what guests keep mentioning

One review doesn't say much. A hundred do. Stamgæst reads them all and groups what guests bring up again and again, so you can see what's working and what isn't.
